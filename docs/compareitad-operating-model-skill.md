# CompareITAD Operating Model Skill

## Purpose

Use this guide whenever working on CompareITAD strategy, copy, outreach, pitch decks, site updates, sales material, product requirements, lead-scoring, or partner/vendor/client workflows.

The critical rule: **CompareITAD is not just a directory.** The directory is one surface of a larger IT asset disposition orchestration, chain-of-custody, vendor-selection, compliance, and resale/value-recovery service.

## Core definition

CompareITAD manages enterprise IT asset disposition on behalf of the asset owner.

It helps enterprises and data-center operators safely dispose of servers, GPUs, storage, networking gear, drives, and other tracked assets by:

1. qualifying the client and disposition project,
2. verifying eligible ITAD vendors and facilities,
3. running a blind competitive vendor-bidding process,
4. letting the client choose the provider,
5. enforcing chain-of-custody requirements,
6. reconciling serial numbers from pickup through final disposition,
7. verifying destruction/sanitization evidence,
8. issuing a CompareITAD disposition record,
9. clearing assets for resale,
10. participating in resale/value-recovery economics.

## Commercial model

### Client engagement

A client comes to CompareITAD with a disposition need, for example 1,000 servers, 500 GPUs, drives, storage arrays, networking gear, or other tracked data-center / enterprise IT assets.

CompareITAD charges:

- **$5,000 minimum engagement**, or
- **$12 per chain-of-custody tracked asset**, whichever applies.

Example: 1,000 tracked assets → $12,000 tracking fee.

### Resale/value recovery

After assets are dispositioned correctly and cleared for resale:

- the gear can be sold through the winning ITAD vendor, another ITAD buyer, an exchange, eBay, or other resale channels;
- competitive bids can help set reserve pricing;
- CompareITAD receives **20% of net resale economics**.

### Agent channel

CompareITAD has a partner/agent channel. Qualified agents may include data-center reps, data-center employees, infrastructure professionals, people close to enterprise refresh cycles, and professionals who know companies that need hardware disposition.

If an agent introduces a qualified disposition opportunity that closes, the agent earns **30% of CompareITAD’s economics on the deal**.

## Vendor model

ITAD vendors must be qualified before participating. They must have appropriate certifications, compliant facilities, validated capabilities, a signed CompareITAD agreement, and agreement to provide required chain-of-custody records.

Signing the agreement does **not** make a vendor preferred. It makes them eligible.

CompareITAD remains neutral by controlling the guardrails, not the outcome:

> CompareITAD verifies vendor eligibility and process compliance. It does not push a preferred vendor. Vendors bid blind, qualified vendors may be recommended, and the client chooses the provider.

## Blind-bid process

1. Client engages CompareITAD and identifies the asset disposition need.
2. CompareITAD verifies the project and requirements.
3. CompareITAD identifies qualified ITAD vendors who meet the client’s needs.
4. Vendors bid without knowing the client’s identity at the initial blind-bid stage.
5. CompareITAD may recommend up to three qualified vendors.
6. The client reviews the bids and chooses.
7. CompareITAD remains neutral because the client makes the vendor decision.

## Chain-of-custody workflow

1. **Origin inventory / data-center scan** — assets are identified and serials captured before leaving the client site.
2. **Truck/load scan** — assets are scanned as they are loaded for transport.
3. **Bill of lading** — transport documentation is collected.
4. **Facility receipt/offload scan** — assets are scanned when received by the ITAD facility.
5. **Serial reconciliation** — CompareITAD’s backend reconciles serial numbers at each stage.
6. **Sanitization/destruction evidence** — ITAD vendor provides proof of wiping, sanitization, destruction, or other required disposition handling.
7. **Certificate of indemnity** — documentation is collected where applicable.
8. **Certificate of destruction** — certificate serials must match the original asset list and custody records.
9. **CompareITAD verification** — CompareITAD validates that the asset trail, serials, documents, and certificates reconcile.
10. **Record of disposition** — CompareITAD issues its own disposition record/certificate for the client.
11. **Resale clearance** — assets are cleared for sale only after compliance and documentation are verified.

## Client value proposition

CompareITAD acts like an independent ITAD journey manager for the asset owner. It does not perform the disposition itself, but it makes sure the right qualified provider is selected, the process is tracked, the records reconcile, and the client can defend its due diligence.

Client benefits:

- reduces compliance risk,
- reduces risk of bad vendor selection,
- avoids babysitting the ITAD vendor,
- creates defensible documentation,
- verifies that assets leaving the data center are the assets sanitized/destroyed,
- supports auditor/regulator/customer due diligence,
- supports resale/value recovery.

## What to avoid

Avoid saying:

- CompareITAD is just a directory.
- CompareITAD recommends preferred vendors.
- CompareITAD is neutral because it does not manage the process.
- CompareITAD is mainly a content farm.
- CompareITAD is only lead generation.
- CompareITAD is only a marketplace.
- Vendors pay to become preferred.

Correct these to:

- CompareITAD is a managed process/service.
- CompareITAD controls eligibility and guardrails.
- Clients choose vendors.
- Vendors sign agreements to participate under CompareITAD rules.
- CompareITAD verifies the process and records.
- CompareITAD clears assets for resale after reconciliation.
