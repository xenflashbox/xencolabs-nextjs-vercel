# Local Service Business OS — launch and outbound plan

Date: 2026-10-08

## Product

Name: **XencoLabs Local Service Business OS**

Promise: **You do the work. The system handles the office.**

Standard:
- $14,500 setup
- $995/month managed platform
- typical target: 2–4 weeks after intake

Founding cohort:
- first 10 tightly scoped deployments
- $10,000 setup
- $750/month

Optional Brand Launch:
- from $3,500
- naming / domain direction / logo / visual identity / field identity guidance

Third-party usage and payment processing stay transparent / pass-through where appropriate.

## Sale structure

Recommended:
1. 20-minute fit / workflow call — no charge
2. written implementation scope
3. 35% kickoff payment
4. 35% at working workflow / design acceptance
5. 30% at launch
6. monthly managed fee begins at production launch

If a self-service "reserve a build slot" flow is added later, use a clearly documented credited reservation payment and signed terms before work begins.

## Lead scoring

Score 0–2 on each:
- owner-operated / small team
- weak or dated website
- phone-only or generic contact-form intake
- no online estimate / quote
- no online booking
- no customer portal
- no recurring-service controls
- weak local SEO / incomplete GBP
- slow follow-up signals
- high review count or visible demand but weak operations

Top accounts get a personalized operating-leak brief.

## Local outbound lanes

### Lane B — 40/day
- personalized email

### Lane C — partner/referral
Target:
- accountants / bookkeepers for trades
- local marketing agencies that do not build operations
- equipment dealers
- insurance brokers
- chambers / trade associations
- commercial property managers

Offer a referral fee only after legal/accounting review and written partner terms.

## Paid acquisition

### For Sonoma Wash Co customers
Priority:
1. Google Business Profile
2. Google Search ads around high-intent service + city / near-me terms
3. eligible Google pay-per-lead / Local Services-style campaign only if the exact business category is supported
4. Meta retargeting / before-after creative
5. cold Meta only after conversion tracking has enough signal

Do not misclassify the business under an unrelated eligible Local Services category merely to get access.

Initial Search test:
- service area only
- call + quote-complete + booking conversions
- high-intent terms
- strong negative list: jobs, salary, DIY, equipment, rental, machine, used pressure washer, how to start business
- start modestly and optimize by booked-job economics rather than clicks

### For XencoLabs Local Service Business OS
Priority:
1. direct outbound using the live case study
2. warm local owner network / referrals
3. retargeting visitors who watched the product demo
4. small Meta founder/owner creative test
5. paid search only after landing page conversion baseline exists

Cold Facebook should be a test, not the primary acquisition engine for a $10K–$15K implementation.

## Voice demo

Create a separate Xenco Labs Local Service Business OS voice number.

Do not use the live Sonoma Wash customer line as the sales demo line because demo traffic can pollute real operations and bookings.

Agent role:
- explain the product
- answer package questions
- qualify business type / employee count / services / current admin pain
- book 20-minute consultation
- never promise scope or final price beyond published package ranges
- hand off unusual integration/compliance questions

Public CTA:
**Call our AI receptionist. Ask it what the system would automate in your business.**

The sales agent itself becomes proof of the product.
