# Local Service Business OS — narrated product pitch brief

Date: 2026-10-08
Reference implementation: Sonoma Wash Co.
Narrator: Marcus Cole
Format: presentation-style, 16:9, fixed-layout stills + Golden Motion Recipe v1 once motion registration QA is finalized.

## Positioning

**You do the work. The system handles the office.**

The product is not a website package. It is a connected operating system for owner-operated local service businesses: customer acquisition, AI phone intake, estimating, scheduling, owner verification, contracts, field workflow, documentation, invoicing, payment and follow-up.

## Recommended deck: 8 slides

### 1 — The owner is the back office
Headline: **Your best technician should not spend every night doing clerical work.**

Visual:
- owner/field operator at center
- incoming calls, estimates, appointments, paperwork, invoices, collections orbiting around them
- simple red "ADMIN BOTTLENECK" marker

Marcus:
"Most local service companies do not have a workmanship problem. They have an operating-leverage problem. The owner is also the receptionist, estimator, scheduler, contract desk, collections team and follow-up system. Every hour spent there is an hour not spent doing the work or selling the next job."

### 2 — One connected operating loop
Headline: **From first call to paid invoice — one system.**

Visual flow:
Lead → Answer → Estimate → Book → Verify → Contract → Job → Document → Invoice → Pay → Follow-up

Marcus:
"Xenco Labs connects the entire customer journey. A lead can arrive by phone or web, get useful answers, price common work, book an assessment, receive a verified scope and contract, move into a field checklist, document completion and receive the final invoice without rebuilding the same customer record at every step."

### 3 — The AI receptionist is useful, not decorative
Headline: **An answering service takes messages. This one knows the business.**

Visual:
Left: generic answering service: Name / Number / "Someone will call you back"
Right: AI receptionist: service Q&A / territory check / live schedule / appointment booking / CRM record

Marcus:
"A traditional answering service can collect a name and number. A knowledge-grounded receptionist can answer the questions customers actually call with, understand the services, enforce the service area, check live availability and book the right appointment. The owner gets the appointment instead of a voicemail to return."

### 4 — Live proof: Sonoma Wash Co.
Headline: **We built the reference business from zero.**

Visual:
- Sonoma Wash Co. live site
- brand mark / van identity
- instant quote
- booking
- admin quote verification
- e-sign
- job checklist
- before/after
- invoice/payment

Marcus:
"Sonoma Wash Co. started without a name, brand, domain or back office. We built the market-facing brand and the operating workflow behind it. The owner can focus on inspecting properties and doing the work while the system handles the repetitive office steps around each job."

### 5 — Growth is part of the system
Headline: **The back office only matters if customers can find you.**

Visual:
Google Maps/GBP → service pages → local pages → problem-intent content → quote / call / booking

Marcus:
"The operating system includes the acquisition foundation too: local search architecture, Google Business Profile, service and problem pages, transparent pricing, conversion tracking and the corpus for ongoing content. The same knowledge that helps Google understand the business also powers the phone agent and follow-up."

### 6 — What gets installed
Headline: **A working system, configured around your business.**

Visual: four grouped pillars
- ACQUIRE: site, local SEO, phone
- SELL: pricing, quote, booking, verification, contract
- DELIVER: tasks, photos, completion
- COLLECT + RETAIN: invoices, Stripe, portal, recurring, CRM nurture

Marcus:
"We do not hand you a pile of subscriptions and ask you to integrate them. We configure the workflow around your service menu, rate logic, territory, scheduling rules and field process, then connect the pieces so the same job moves cleanly from lead to payment."

### 7 — Package and timeline
Headline: **Launch in weeks, not another year of software projects.**

Visual:
Founding cohort: $10,000 setup + $750/mo
Standard: $14,500 setup + $995/mo
Brand Launch add-on: from $3,500
Typical target: 2–4 weeks after intake

Marcus:
"The standard implementation starts at fourteen-five with ongoing management from nine-ninety-five a month. The first tightly scoped founding deployments are ten thousand plus seven-fifty monthly while we expand the reference architecture into additional service verticals. Businesses starting from zero can add naming and branding."

### 8 — Call to action
Headline: **Show us how your business works today.**

Visual:
15–20 minute consultation → workflow map → fit decision → build slot

Primary CTA: Book a Build Consultation
Secondary: See Sonoma Wash Co. live

Marcus:
"Bring us your services, pricing, territory and the office work that keeps following you home. In one conversation we can tell you what fits the operating system, what still needs human judgment and what a deployment would look like. If the fit is right, we reserve the build and get moving."
