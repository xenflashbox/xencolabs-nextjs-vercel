## Corpus

Seed from:
1. Sonoma Wash Co. current site as first-party approved business knowledge
2. Manufacturer/SDS material for cleaning chemistry where claims are made
3. Surface-manufacturer guidance (roofing, concrete, siding, wood) for safe-treatment claims
4. California / local stormwater and wastewater guidance for commercial runoff claims

Every extracted fact should keep source URL, retrieved_at and verification date.

## Google Business Profile

Set up immediately as a service-area business if customers are not served at the business address:
- real-world name: Sonoma Wash Co.
- hide the address if no customer-facing storefront
- primary category: Pressure washing service (confirm in live selector)
- secondary category only when genuinely offered, e.g. Gutter cleaning service
- do not use Roofing contractor merely because roof cleaning is offered
- use specific city/postal service areas, not a radius
- keep the service-area list aligned to the actual published travel zones
- load real services and pricing descriptions
- use the website and the dedicated business phone
- add authentic job, equipment, van/team and before/after media
- start review requests immediately after successful real jobs
- respond to every review in the business's normal voice

Do not upload AI-generated or mockup field photos as if they are real operating evidence.

## Measurement

Track:
- GBP calls, website clicks, direction/service interactions
- quote starts / quote completes
- assessment booking
- phone-agent call → booking
- quote → verified scope
- contract sent / signed
- job completed
- invoice paid

The advantage of this build is that local SEO can be measured through the entire revenue workflow, not only rankings.
